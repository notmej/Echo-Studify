
You can generate a release keystore like this:
keytool -genkeypair -v \
-keystore studify-release-key.jks \
-keyalg RSA \
-keysize 2048 \
-validity 10000 \
-alias studify


public certificate fingerprints for your local Android build:
keytool -list -v \
-keystore ~/.android/debug.keystore \
-alias androiddebugkey \
-storepass android \
-keypass android
